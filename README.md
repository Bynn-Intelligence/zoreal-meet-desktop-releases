# ZOREAL Meet for Mac and Windows

ZOREAL Meet is identity-verified meetings and calls. The desktop app brings your meetings and
calls to your computer:

- Meetings in a window of their own, signed in with a passkey, Google, Apple, email and password,
  or Sign in with ZOREAL.
- Calls ring on your computer even with the window closed, and you choose to answer with video or
  audio only.
- During a call the window shrinks to a small one you can keep in sight while you work.
- Share a window or your whole screen.
- Meeting links open straight in the app.
- The app keeps itself up to date.

Each release also carries a Mac installer package (`.pkg`) for deployment with your device
management, and templates for managed settings.

## Verifying a download

The Mac app is signed with our Developer ID (Bynn Intelligence, Inc.) and notarized by Apple.
Each release lists the SHA-256 of every file in `SHA256SUMS`:

```sh
shasum -a 256 ZOREAL-Meet-mac.dmg
```
